Founded in 2012, Lumière has spent over a decade refining the art of interior composition — blending architecture, materiality, and the lived experience of space.

Who We Are

A studio built on the belief that space is the most intimate art.

Lumière was born from a simple conviction: that interiors have the power to transform how we feel, how we connect, and how we live. We approach every project as a collaboration — listening deeply before we draw a single line.

Our process is meticulous and unhurried. We begin with stories, not floor plans — understanding who you are and how you want to inhabit your space. From there, we curate materials, light, and form into something that feels entirely yours.

We partner with the finest craftspeople, architects, and artisans — people who share our commitment to quality and longevity. Every element is considered, from the joinery detail to the final layer of paint.
