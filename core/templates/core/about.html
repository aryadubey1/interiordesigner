Space is the most intimate art.

Lumière began with a simple conviction: interiors change how we feel, how we connect and how we live. Every project is a collaboration, and we listen before we draw a single line.

Our process is careful and unhurried. We start with stories, not floor plans, and learn how you want to live in a space. Then we bring material, light and form together into something that feels entirely yours.

We work with fine craftspeople, architects and artisans who share our commitment to quality and longevity, from the joinery detail to the last coat of paint.

What guides us

Intention

Every decision is deliberate. We don't fill space; we shape it, and each element earns its place.

Materiality

Stone, brass, timber, linen: materials that age beautifully and grow richer with time.

Collaboration

A great interior is a dialogue. We listen as much as we create, and build trust at every stage.
